Maison History / Paris 8ème Arrondissement / Since 2018

The Discipline of Permanent Form.

Atelier Éternel was founded on a singular conviction: that true luxury is not defined by seasonal novelty, but by the relentless refinement of volumetric drape, raw natural fibers, and master pattern-cutting.

Figure 01: Master Cutter Hands · Paris Cutting Room
The Physical Workshop

Where Modernism Meets Centuries of Savoir-Faire

Located in a discreet courtyard on the Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, our Paris atelier operates in direct defiance of mass apparel systems. Here, there are no conveyor lines or digital automated laser presses.

Patterns are drawn directly on untreated virgin paper using tailor’s chalk and horn-weighted rulers. Each overcoat requires up to 140 uninterrupted hours of hand-guided needlework, pad-stitching, and cold-water sponge pressing.

Location: 24 Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, Paris
Staff: 12 Master Tailors & Apprentice Cutters
Production Cap: Max 45 pieces per edition
Chronicle

The Three Pillars

Pillar I · 2018

The Architectural Origin

Founded by architectural designer and master tailor Jean-Philippe Moreau following a decade in bespoke Savile Row and Parisian haute couture houses. The atelier was born as a rejection of disposable trend cycles in favor of brutalist volumetric permanence.

Milestone: First Archival Cashmere Overcoat #001
Pillar II · Present

The Craftsmanship Covenant

Every single garment is fully or half-canvassed using genuine floating horsehair and Irish linen canvas. We do not use glued synthetic fusible interfacings, allowing the chest to contour to the wearer’s anatomy and breath naturally over decades.

Standard: 140 Hours Average Needlework Per Piece
Pillar III · Ongoing

The Zero-Waste Commitment

We operate strictly on small numbered runs of 45 pieces. Offcuts from cashmere overcoats are salvaged and spun into insulating inner cuffs or handcrafted leather clutch pouches. Unsold stock is never incinerated or discounted; it is preserved in our private archive.

Purity: 100% GOTS & LWG Gold-Certified Fibers

Fibers of Singular Origin

We partner with generational farming families and historic European mills who treat textile harvesting as a sacred dialogue with landscape.

01 / Cashmere

Mongolian Grade-A

Harvested by hand-combing during the spring molt in the Bayankhongor steppes. 15-micron fineness spun exclusively in Biella, Italy.

Origin: Ulaanbaatar & Biella
02 / Wool Fresco

Super 130s High-Twist

Woven from non-mulesed Saxon merino wool. High-twist double yarn creates a crisp, crease-resistant drape with natural thermal breathability.

Origin: Yorkshire & Biella
03 / Calfskin

French Vegetable-Tanned

Full-grain hides conditioned with mimosa and chestnut tree tannins. Zero chromium salts. Polished with natural carnauba wax.

Origin: Alsace & Florence
04 / Gabardine

Lancashire Cotton

Tightly packed 2-ply Egyptian long-staple cotton yarn. Treated with eco-finish water resistance that preserves natural cotton hand.

Origin: Lancashire, UK
