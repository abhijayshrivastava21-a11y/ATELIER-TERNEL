House Protocols & Inquiries

Made-to-measure tailoring and bespoke overcoats require approximately 4 to 6 weeks from anatomical measurement to final fitting. This allows for intermediate basted fitting sessions and thorough hand-steaming on shaped wooden forms.

Because we only weave our virgin wools and cashmere in batches matching limited pastoral wool yields, our collections are capped at 45 pieces. Once an archival edition is spoken for, it is not re-manufactured until the subsequent calendar year’s spring molt.
